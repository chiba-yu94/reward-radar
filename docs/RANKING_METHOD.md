# Ranking method

The same deterministic ordering runs on both languages. Verified country filters apply as hard exclusions, and explicitly accepting Japan requires Japan to appear in a verified country list. Stale entries follow fresh entries. There is no placement advantage for affiliate URLs.

Guaranteed rewards lead the consumer directory. Amounts qualify for net comparison when the reward and required expense share a currency, or required spending is zero. The ordering groups comparable entries by currency and sorts by descending net within a group. The next keys are required expense class, estimated time, redemption difficulty, verified geography, deadline, and stable record ID. Unknown values are ordered after known values where that comparison applies. No cross-currency conversion is performed.

Sweepstakes order uses deadlines, expense class and ID. Prize amounts are the advertised amounts and no expected value or probability is derived. Other consumer rewards use expense class, deadline, geography and ID.

Affiliate programs appear separately. The order uses stale status, commission structure, descending known percent, descending known duration, eligibility verification and ID. Payment thresholds are displayed in their original currency where known. Promotion restrictions and application terms appear alongside the official evidence. Rates and duration alone do not establish earned income.

Public explanations are available at `/ja/ranking/` and `/en/ranking/`.
